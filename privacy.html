Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TekOmni LLC ("TekOmni," "we," "us," or "our"), operating the GoFleetAI transportation management platform ("Service"), collects, uses, shares, and protects information. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to provide fleet dispatch, tracking, compliance, and accounting features:

  • Account & contact information — names, email addresses, mobile phone numbers, business details, and roles for the dispatchers, fleet managers, and drivers who use the Service.
  • Operational data — loads, stops, dispatch assignments, GPS/ELD telemetry, Hours of Service data, documents (e.g., BOLs, rate confirmations), and accounting records.
  • Usage & device data — log data, IP address, browser/device type, and interactions with the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the Service — dispatch loads, track vehicles, monitor compliance, process invoicing and settlement.
  • To send operational notifications (in-app, email, and SMS) such as load dispatch alerts.
  • To secure, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations (e.g., FMCSA, DOT).

3. SMS / Text Messaging

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in, or consent information with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used solely to deliver the messaging described below.

GoFleetAI sends operational SMS text messages to drivers and personnel associated with our customers' fleets — for example, a load dispatch notification that includes a load reference number and a link to the load details in the driver application.

Consent

Mobile phone numbers are provided by our customers (fleet operators) on behalf of their drivers and personnel, or by individuals directly, for the purpose of receiving these operational notifications. By providing a mobile number, you consent to receive these messages.

Message frequency & rates

Message frequency varies with dispatch and operational activity. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out and help

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message; you will receive a confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at TellMeMore@TekOmni.com.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Service providers — vetted vendors that power the platform (e.g., cloud hosting, ELD/telematics, messaging, mapping, accounting integrations) under contracts that restrict their use of the data to providing services to us.
  • Your organization — data is shared among authorized users within your fleet/company account.
  • Legal & safety — when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information, and as stated above we do not share mobile opt-in or consent data with third parties for their marketing.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations (including transportation recordkeeping requirements), resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and tenant data isolation. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and continuously improve our safeguards. See our Security page for more.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP.
  • Update or correct account information through the Service or by contacting us.
  • Request access to or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and contractual retention requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, by additional notice.
