See it working

Real screens from the app, with sample data. Select any screen to open it full size.

Tenders turn into loads

Rate confirmations and load tenders that land in your dispatch inbox are read by AI and turned into draft loads. A person reviews each one before it is booked.

  • Each offer shows whether a truck can cover it and how far out it is.
  • A revised tender is linked to the load it changes, never booked twice.
  • Nobody retypes a rate con, so the invoice matches it.

Every load on one screen

Stops, charges, expenses, driver pay and projected profit sit on one page. Change a charge and the pay and margin move with it.

  • Revenue, driver pay, fuel and margin before the truck rolls.
  • Relay loads split into segments, each with its own driver and pay.
  • Warnings when something is off, like a trailer that isn't with its truck.

The driver's phone runs the stop

Drivers see one next step at a time: accept the load, drive to the pickup, arrive, add the POD. In English or Spanish.

  • Arrivals are confirmed by the truck's GPS, the trailer's tracker or the phone.
  • The POD is captured at the dock, so you can bill the same day.
  • Drivers message dispatch and report problems from the same app.

Pay set up the way each driver is paid

Per mile, percentage, hourly, flat or salary, with deductions, reserve accounts and benefits, set per driver.

  • Settlements are built from the same loads you invoiced.
  • A statement can be corrected and reissued. Both versions stay on file.
  • A spreadsheet error once over-deducted one driver about $11,400 a year. This design ended it.

Bill the day you deliver

Delivered loads move to review, then to invoice. Missing paperwork is called out before anything is sent.

  • Generate and email invoices in one click, or send them to your factoring company.
  • See what is pending, sent, sold to factor and paid at a glance.
  • Detention runs from the appointment time and lands on the load as a charge.

The shop sees problems as they happen

A defect reported from the driver app lands on the shop's board. The shop and dispatch share one calendar, so a truck in the shop isn't dispatched.

  • Engine fault codes from the ELD show up as tickets.
  • Service schedules run by date or by miles, for trucks and trailers.
  • Mechanics clock their shifts at the shop and get paid from the clock.

Yard rent that bills itself

Spare yard space becomes monthly income. Tenants keep a card on file and rent is charged each month, in the same system that runs the trucks.

  • See who has paid, who hasn't, and whose card is expiring.
  • Spots, deposits and balances in one list.
