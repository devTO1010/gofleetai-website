Bill the day you deliver

Detention bills itself

The clock runs from the appointment, dwell is tracked at every stop, and the charge lands on the load. It stops being a favor you forget to invoice.

Pay right the first time

By load, lane, customer, truck, and driver-week — with fuel, tolls, and overhead in the math, not guessed.
