Price every lane on what it really costs you.

Most fleets quote from revenue per mile and find the truth at tax time. GoFleetAI counts the miles no load paid for, and the costs your books already hold.

  • Know your true cost per mile. Each truck's daily miles come from its ELD odometer, so empty miles are in the number. Quote against what a mile actually costs.
  • Truck and trailer maintenance, per mile. Projected maintenance comes from the repair costs of each truck and trailer. It needs your repair shop software connected, or repair bills that name the vehicle.
  • Your QuickBooks costs, already in your margins. Choose which cost categories take their amount from QuickBooks. The books are read every night and each cost lands on the trucks that carried it. No month-end spreadsheet.
  • Map your books in minutes. Assign dozens of QuickBooks accounts to a cost category in one move instead of one at a time.

Hire, pay and keep the people who keep you moving.

Driver turnover and shop labor are where a fleet's margin quietly leaks. Pay that is accurate and explainable is what keeps good people.

  • Every driver, from hire to exit

    Mark each driver Onboarding, In training, Active, Offboarding or Terminated, so everyone knows who can run and who is on the way out. The application is on file the day you hire.

  • Message every owner-operator at once

    One message reaches all company drivers, all owner-operators, all independent contractors or all carrier drivers, each in their own thread. A policy change goes out once, not forty times.

  • Pay mechanics from a real clock

    Mechanics clock in and out from their phone, and a tap counts only at your shop. Hourly or daily pay comes from the clock, so there are no timesheet disputes.

  • Offer staff PTO and medical

    Office and shop staff accrue paid time off and medical every pay period, and approved time off draws it down. Benefits become a reason to join and a reason to stay.

  • Pay commission when the money lands

    Pay dispatchers and sales agents on delivered, invoiced or paid loads, whichever you choose. Choose paid, and no commission goes out on a load the customer never pays for.
