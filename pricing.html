Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a "truck"?
Each active power unit (truck/tractor) in your fleet counts as one truck. Trailers, drivers, and user accounts are unlimited on all plans.
How does the 1–15 truck price work?
You pay $500 a month plus 0.4% of the freight revenue you invoice through GoFleetAI that month. The total never exceeds $1,500 a month. A fleet that invoices $90,000 in a month pays $860. A fleet that invoices $250,000 or more pays the $1,500 maximum.
How does the 16–30 truck price work?
You pay $100 a month for each truck. A fleet of 16 trucks pays $1,600, a fleet of 20 pays $2,000, and a fleet of 30 pays $3,000. There is no revenue share on this tier.
What do 31 or more trucks cost?
$3,000 a month plus $0.01 for each loaded mile on the freight you invoice through GoFleetAI that month. A fleet that invoices 300,000 loaded miles in a month pays $6,000. If you run several companies or need custom integrations, book a pricing conversation and we will quote it with you.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Move up a tier when your fleet grows. A move to a lower tier takes effect at the next billing cycle.
Is there a setup fee?
No setup fee on the 1–15 and 16–30 truck tiers. Larger fleets get custom onboarding and training, priced in the sales conversation.
Do I need an ELD or GPS provider?
For those features, yes. GoFleetAI does not provide ELD or GPS services for trucks, trailers or other equipment. Live locations, arrival detection, hours-of-service clocks, odometer mileage, engine fault codes, IFTA mileage and trailer tracking need a paid third-party ELD or GPS solution, such as Samsara or Motive, which GoFleetAI connects to. Dispatch, pay and invoicing work without one.
How long does onboarding take?
Most fleets are fully operational within 5-7 business days. ELD integration (Samsara) typically connects in under an hour. Data migration from your existing TMS is handled by our team.
Can someone help us automate our operations and build dashboards?
Yes. An AI expert consultant is available as an add-on at an additional cost. They help you automate routine work and build the dashboards and analytics you run the business on. Ask about it during your teardown.
Do you offer a free trial?
We offer a 14-day free trial on all plans. No credit card required. Start with your full fleet or a subset — your choice.
